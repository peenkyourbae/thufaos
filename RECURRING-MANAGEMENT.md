# Recurring Schedule Management

- Delete one occurrence, this and future occurrences, or the entire repeating series.
- When editing a recurring occurrence, choose whether shared details apply to this occurrence, this and future occurrences, or the whole series.
- Each occurrence retains its own date, ID, completion status, and calendar event reference.
- Series changes affect local ThufaOS schedules only; they do not edit/delete events already sent to Apple Calendar.

Choices use a simple prompt (1/2/3) to avoid changing the existing modal layout. Test carefully with a disposable series before relying on it for important schedules.
